# Positioning

## One-liner
**ParceliQ screens Florida vacant land in bulk and kills the bad lots before you waste an evening on them.**

Short (≤10 words): *Upload your land leads. We kill the bad ones.* (current hero — keep it, it's good.)

## Who it's for (in priority order)
1. **Land flippers / wholesalers** mailing thousands of Florida vacant-lot owners. Pain: most of the list is swamp, landlocked, or in a flood zone, and they find out after the seller says yes.
2. **Acquisition teams at small land funds** who get spreadsheets of 100–1,000 parcels and triage by hand.
3. **Builders / infill developers** checking whether a lot can take a house and septic.

Not for (yet): buyers outside Florida; people who need a legal opinion, survey, or title work.

## The problem, in their words
"I pulled a list of 800 vacant lots in Marion County. Checking each one is a FEMA map, the wetland mapper, the appraiser site and Google satellite for road access. That's 30–60 minutes a parcel. I either do 10 a night or I mail blind."

## What we do differently
| They do today | ParceliQ |
|---|---|
| 5 browser tabs per parcel | One upload, 12 checks run at once |
| A gut call | PURSUE / KILL verdict, a 0–100 score, and the reason for every flag |
| Black-box "lot scores" | Every flag drawn on the map and tied to its government source |
| Pay per record | Flat monthly plans, no per-parcel fees (free during beta) |
| Buy a list, then screen it | Land Leads: pick a county on a map and get lots that already passed screening |

## Proof points (use concrete numbers, not adjectives)
- 67 Florida counties, statewide.
- 12 checks per parcel, including flood, wetlands, protected habitat, road access, power, contamination, hurricane evacuation, soil/septic, elevation and conservation easements, plus county owner/valuation records.
- 30–60 minutes by hand → seconds per parcel.
- "A 100-lead list is a week of evenings by hand, or one screening run." (pricing page — strong line, reuse it.)
- Example verdict to show (from the landing demo): *4315 Hagan Rd, Polk City — PURSUE 86. Flood clear (Zone X), road 64 m away; open questions: wetlands within ~100 m and poorly drained soil, so order a wetland line + soil test before offering.* This shows we flag nuance, not just pass/fail.

## Objections and answers
- **"Is the data current?"** Live government sources (FEMA flood, USFWS wetlands & critical habitat, EPA, USDA soils, county property records), re-screen any parcel with one click.
- **"Can I trust a score?"** Don't trust the score — read the reasons. Each one links to the map layer and source.
- **"What does it cost?"** Free during beta, no card. Planned $29/$49/$79 a month, flat, cancel anytime.
- **"Will it replace my due diligence?"** No. It tells you which 20% of the list deserves due diligence, and what to check first.

## Tone
Plain, specific, a little blunt ("we kill the bad ones"). Talk like an investor in a Facebook land group, not a SaaS brochure. Numbers over adjectives. Emerald #059669 as the accent; KILL in red, PURSUE in green.
