About
Why ParceliQ exists

ParceliQ is a screening tool for Florida vacant land, built for investors who work through hundreds of leads at a time. Before this, checking one parcel meant a flood map here, a wetland viewer there, the county appraiser site, and a satellite view for road access. Thirty minutes or more per parcel, every parcel.

ParceliQ runs all of those checks at once and gives you a straight answer: pursue it or kill it, with the reasons spelled out. The bad lots die in seconds. Your time goes to the deals that deserve it.

It covers all 67 Florida counties, uses live government records, and shows its work on every verdict. No black box.

Stop guessing. Start closing better deals.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

ParceliQ ("we," "us," "our") operates parceliq.com and the ParceliQ web application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address, either directly or through Google Sign-In (Google OAuth). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google — we do not receive your Google password or access your other Google data.

Usage data. When you use the Service, we store:

— The property addresses you submit for screening
— The screening results generated for those addresses (score, verdict, and related property data)
— Batch names, notes you add to parcels, and your usage volume (for enforcing free-tier limits)

Automatically collected data. We may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information for security and troubleshooting purposes.

We do not collect payment information at this time, as ParceliQ is currently offered free of charge.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

— Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
— Authenticate your account and secure access
— Generate and cache parcel screening results
— Enforce usage limits during the free beta
— Communicate with you about your account or the Service
— Improve accuracy and performance of the Service

3. How we store your information

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider, hosted in the United States. Supabase provides the infrastructure for our database; we do not sell or share your data with Supabase for their own purposes.

Property screening results may be cached for up to 24 hours to improve performance across users searching the same address. Cached results are limited to publicly available government property data (flood zones, wetlands, zoning, etc.) and are not tied to any individual user.

4. Third-party services

ParceliQ retrieves property data from public government and open data sources and third-party APIs used solely to process your requests. We do not share your personal account information (name, email) with these services — only the property addresses necessary to generate a screening result.

We use Google OAuth for optional sign-in. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. What we don't do

— We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
— We do not use your data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
— We do not share the addresses you screen with other users, except where you explicitly choose to share a batch via a public link.

6. Shared batches

If you use the "share batch" feature, a public link is generated that allows anyone with the link to view that batch's results. Do not share this link if you do not want the contents visible to others. Shared links expire automatically after 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your account information and screening history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 10).
