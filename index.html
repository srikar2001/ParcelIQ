About
Why ParceliQ exists

ParceliQ is a screening tool for Florida vacant land, built for investors who work through hundreds of leads at a time. Before this, checking one parcel meant a flood map here, a wetland viewer there, the county appraiser site, and a satellite view for road access. Thirty minutes or more per parcel, every parcel.

ParceliQ runs all of those checks at once and gives you a straight answer: pursue it or kill it, with the reasons spelled out. The bad lots die in seconds. Your time goes to the deals that deserve it.

It covers all 67 Florida counties, uses live government records, and shows its work on every verdict. No black box.

Stop guessing. Start closing better deals.
Join land investors and acquisition teams using ParceliQ to save time, reduce risk, and find the right lots.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

1. What we collect

When you use ParceliQ we collect: your email address and name (at signup), the property addresses and parcel numbers you screen, your screening results and saved batches, notes you attach to parcels, and basic usage data (batches run, parcels screened, feature usage) needed to operate limits and improve the product.

2. How we use it

We use your data to deliver the Service: running screenings, saving your batch history, enforcing usage limits, providing support, and improving reliability. We do not sell your personal information or your screened-address lists to anyone. Your lead lists are your business, and we treat them as confidential.

3. Where it lives

Account data and screening results are stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider), which acts as a data processor on our behalf. Screening requests are processed by our backend hosting provider. Property addresses you screen are sent to third-party geocoding and public government data services solely to produce your results.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences (theme, sort order), and cache recent results for speed. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

5. Your rights

You can access your data in the app at any time. You may request a copy of your data or deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

6. Retention

We keep your account data and batch history while your account is active. Cached screening results expire automatically. If you delete your account, associated personal data is deleted within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or security reasons.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or requests? Use the contact form.
